Why we’re different

The listing comes to you

Marketplaces make you scroll. Here you describe the item once and sellers compete to match it.

Built for rare things

Discontinued, limited or out-of-print items rarely have a listing. They do have owners.

Nothing staged

Every request and offer on this page was posted by a real person. Empty means nobody has posted yet.

Brand notes: five names considered
  • Sought — short, a real word that means wanted. Chosen.
  • Lodestar — evocative, but long and common in other industries.
  • Wantly — clear, but reads as a coupon app.
  • Quarry — fits hunting, but suggests mining.
  • Beacon — friendly, but heavily used already.

About Sought

Sought exists to solve one problem: finding a specific, rare or discontinued product. Instead of searching everywhere, a buyer describes the item once and the people who own it can respond.
